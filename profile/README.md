MacX MediaTrans 8.6 is developed by MacXDVD Software, and it’s a Mac-based iOS content manager for transferring and organizing photos, music, videos, podcasts, audiobooks, and general files between iPhone, iPad, iPod, and Mac, without going through iTunes at all. Aiseesoft FoneLab for Android covers the Android side of device management, and MediaTrans fills that same role specifically for Apple devices.

Speed is one of the software’s standout claims: transferring 100 4K photos takes around 8 seconds, according to the developer, a meaningful difference from iTunes’ typically slow, all-or-nothing sync process. HEIC to JPG conversion happens automatically during transfer too, useful for viewing iPhone photos on non-Apple devices without a separate conversion step.

Video handling goes beyond simple file copying. MediaTrans transcodes formats like MKV, AVI, MTS, and HEVC into something an iPhone or iPad can actually play, since iOS doesn’t natively support several common video formats. It can also cut file size by up to 50% during transfer and correct video orientation for sideways footage. The app doubles as a secure flash-drive-style backup tool too, letting you store general files, Word documents, PDFs, even other apps, directly on your iPhone’s storage as if it were a USB drive.
